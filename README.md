# Full House

Most small venues have one night a week that just doesn't move. A cafe, a bar, a bookshop, a record shop. The owner knows it's slow but not what would bring people in.

Full House looks at the street around the venue through Qloo's taste data and works out what the people nearby are into: the artists, films, books and places they already love. Then it plans one night for that slow slot. Who to feature, which places nearby to team up with, what to pour. The owner says yes, and it puts the night out: a public event page, a poster, calendar entries and the partner emails ready to send.

Every Monday it looks again and plans the next one, using how the last night went.

Early days. This is the Worker skeleton and the shared types so far. The Qloo client comes next.

Built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon.
